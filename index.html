Starter Bench Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Dev Jang

What the app stores

Starter Bench saves your feeding weights, times, notes, and rise measurements in its private app storage on your Android device. The app uses this information to display your journal and graphs. The calculator processes your inputs on the device.

Collection and sharing

Starter Bench has no account, ads, analytics, or server connection. The app does not transmit the records to Dev Jang or any third party.

Retention and deletion

Records stay on your device until you delete them. Delete individual records from the journal or clear Starter Bench storage in Android settings to remove all records. Android backup is disabled for the app.
